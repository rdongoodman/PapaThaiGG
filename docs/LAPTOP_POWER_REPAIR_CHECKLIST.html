Gigabyte 4K Laptop — Power Repair

Aug 6, 2026 · RTX 3070 · 64 GB · No power ~2.5 yrs · Step-by-step with Cursor + photos
First session: Step 1.2 only — meter adapter tip (NOT laptop). Photo meter + label → Cursor.

Phase 1 — Power brick (no laptop)

  • Visual: melt, smell, bent tip, label V/W
  • Meter DC at barrel: center = red, sleeve = black
  • ~19 V stable = OK · 0 V = bad brick/outlet · wrong V = do not use
  • Wiggle test: voltage drops = bad cable

Phase 2 — Laptop external

  • Battery disconnect if possible
  • Adapter in → power button → any LED/fan?
  • Check DC jack: loose, burned, wiggle

Phase 3 — Open bottom

  • Model sticker photo · service manual
  • Look: burns, swollen caps, blown fuse, corrosion
  • Reseat battery connector · retry

Phase 4 — Inside meter (Cursor guides)

Jack pads · fuse continuity · no battery test · power button ribbon — probe only when Cursor marks your board from photos.

Phase 5 — Realistic outcomes

SignLikely
0 V at adapterNew adapter
OK adapter, loose jackResolder jack
Blown fuse, no other burnReplace fuse
Burned chipBoard repair or used motherboard
LEDs on, no bootNot power — RAM/display

Phase 6 — Stop / shop

Large burn, confused readings, micro-solder needed → local shop quote with adapter + photos.
